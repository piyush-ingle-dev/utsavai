## Book the venue and caterer first | all | tip
These two bookings fix most of the budget and the date, so lock them before anything else. Ask for written quotes that include taxes, overtime and cleaning charges.

## Hidden venue costs | wedding,birthday | risk
Overtime, generator, parking, cleaning and decoration deposits often arrive as extras. Ask for an all-inclusive quote in writing and read the cancellation terms.

## Caterer headcount and extra plates | wedding,birthday | tip
Confirm the final guest count one to two weeks ahead. Ask how extra plates are billed, and keep around 10 percent spare capacity for walk-ins as a common rule of thumb.

## Dietary needs | all | tip
Collect dietary needs in the invitation or RSVP. Use separate counters, utensils and serving staff for different dietary needs, and label every dish clearly.

## Muhurat and ritual timing | wedding | tip
Fix the muhurat with the priest before booking the venue and photographer, because everything else is scheduled around it. Ask for the ritual items list early so nothing is missing on the day.

## Sangeet and haldi | wedding | idea
Short, well-rehearsed family performances with a hosted emcee keep a sangeet lively. Hold the haldi in a washable outdoor area and keep spare clothes and towels ready.

## Outstation guests | wedding | tip
Block hotel rooms early, share a pickup schedule and give each guest a welcome kit with the schedule, a map and one contact number.

## Keep a contingency | all | risk
Keep 5 to 10 percent of the budget unallocated. Last-minute changes and price rises are the most common reason for overspending.

## Rain and heat plan | all | risk
For outdoor events arrange a covered backup area, shade, drinking water and fans or coolers. Decide the day before who calls the switch to the backup.

## Photographer brief | wedding,birthday | tip
Share a must-have shot list and the key family groups with the photographer. Ask about delivery time, raw files and backup copies.

## Sound and music | all | tip
Do a sound check one day before. Keep volume comfortable for elderly guests, and check local rules on loudspeakers, which usually restrict late-night use.

## Kids party flow | birthday | idea
Keep it to arrival, games, food and cake in about two to three hours. Short games led by a host avoid chaos and keep children engaged.

## Useful return gifts | birthday | idea
Seed packets, notebooks or story books are remembered longer than plastic toys and create less waste.

## Theme with one hero element | all | idea
Choose two main colours and one hero element, such as an entrance arch or a photo wall, instead of decorating everything. It looks better and costs less.

## Cake logistics | birthday | tip
Order the cake three to five days ahead, confirm eggless or allergy needs, and plan transport and a cool storage spot on the day.

## Permissions for community events | community | tip
Apply early to the local municipal body and police for the venue, pandal and loudspeakers, and get any society NOC. Keep copies of every permission at the venue.

## Fire and crowd safety | community | risk
Keep exits clear, place fire extinguishers and first aid, use fire-safe decoration material and assign volunteers to manage queues and crowd flow.

## Eco-friendly Ganesh celebrations | community | idea
Prefer natural clay idols, cloth and paper decor and steel plates. Plan immersion as per local civic guidance, since many cities offer artificial tanks or collection points.

## Volunteer management | community | tip
Give every volunteer a named lead, a two-hour shift and a one-page role sheet. Hold a short briefing before the doors open.

## Collecting contributions transparently | community | tip
Record every contribution in a shared sheet and publish a simple income and expense summary after the event. Transparency builds trust for next year.

## Prasad and food at scale | community | tip
Estimate quantity per head, use serving counters with trained volunteers, provide clean water and handwashing, and plan waste collection. Keep food covered and served hot.

## Cultural programme planning | community | idea
Mix local performers, children's items and short speeches. Fix slot timings, rehearse once and keep a buffer so the programme ends on time.

## Budget per guest sanity check | all | tip
Divide the total budget by the number of guests. If catering alone would take far more than a third of that amount, reduce guests or simplify the menu before booking anything.

## Digital invitations | all | idea
A digital invitation with a map pin, schedule and RSVP link saves printing cost and gets faster replies. Send a reminder two days before.

## Day-of coordinator | all | tip
Name one person who answers all vendor questions on the day so the family can enjoy the event. Share a one-page schedule with every vendor.

## Decor on a budget | all | tip
Reuse flowers and props across functions and choose seasonal flowers. Ask the decorator which items are rented, what is extra and how long teardown takes.

## Elderly guests | all | tip
Provide seating, easy access, shade and a quiet area, plus a volunteer to assist them.
